Cinderquay — a 5v5 round-based tactical FPS in three.js: plant/defuse, buy economy, bots. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best three.js tactical FPS” bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

A container terminal at dusk. Five on five, first to five rounds — plant the charge at site A or B, or wipe the other team out. Stop to aim: firing on the move sprays wide. Five weapons, two grenades, a buy economy between rounds, and bots that push lanes, hold angles, plant, defuse, and react to what they see and hear.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8901 in desktop Chrome. Click to play: WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, E to plant/defuse, B for the buy menu, Tab for the scoreboard.
